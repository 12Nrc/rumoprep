When to start

The spring of junior year is ideal, so the personal statement gets done over the summer, before the busy fall. Full application support closes to new students on September 1, because writing together takes months. Essay review stays open all year for late starters, regular decision essays and waitlist letters.

Questions

Common questions

Something else on your mind? Ask me directly

How much does it cost?

Coaching is priced per year, not by the hour, so there are no surprise bills. The cost depends on the support your student needs, and I'll walk you through it in the free consultation.

Will you write my student's essays?

No. Your student writes every word. I help brainstorm, structure and give detailed feedback, which is what professional ethics guidelines for college counselors call for.

Can you guarantee admission?

No one honestly can. I can help your student put forward the strongest, clearest application they're capable of.

Do we meet in person or online?

Most meetings are on video, which keeps scheduling easy. In-person meetings in the DC area are possible during school breaks.

Do you help with athletic recruiting?

No. Athletes are welcome for the standard academic application, but recruiting, coach outreach and NCAA questions are outside what I offer.

Do you help with financial aid?

I can explain the basics of the FAFSA and CSS Profile and help compare offers. For detailed financial aid planning, I'll point you to a specialist.
